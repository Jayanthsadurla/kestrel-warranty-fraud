**Subject:** Warranty fraud check: what to do next week

**The decision.** Use the tool to pick which claims the investigation desk looks at each month. Do not use it to refuse claims. A flag means "look at this one first", not "this is fraud".

**On the 97% target.** Please do not hold us to it. Only about 1 claim in 30 is fraud now, so a tool that flags nothing is already about 97% "accurate" and stops no fraud. Our tool, which really does catch fraud, scores slightly lower on that measure. A better number for the board: **how much fraud we stop per claim checked.**

**The number.** We tested on June claims, which the tool had never seen. If the desk checks the 38 claims the tool ranks highest (a 40-a-month desk):
- It finds **15 of the 22 fraudulent claims** that month (about 7 in 10).
- It stops about **Rs 20,900 of fraud**, which is **Rs 550 for every claim checked**.
- It wrongly holds about 23 honest customers (about Rs 8,700 in goodwill), and reviews cost roughly Rs 9,900.
- **Net gain: about Rs 2,000 to 3,000 a month.** Small. The tool is worth having, but it is not where the big money is.

**Where the money is: the 1 May rule.** Since claims under Rs 2,000 were approved without inspection, fraud has moved there. Fraud is now about 1 claim in 40, and still roughly Rs 35,000 to 45,000 a month, nearly all of it in small claims from a handful of newer partners.

**Your view on newer partners: half right.** Partners that joined in the last year have 7% fraudulent claims, against 1% for the rest. But only 8 of 46 such partners have any fraud at all. Among new partners' claims under Rs 2,000 since May, 1 in 5 was fraud. Meenal is right that most new partners are fine. We would act on the few, not the group.

**What to do next week**
1. **Put the 8 partners with the most flags back on full inspection** (we can give the list). This is cheaper and fairer than flagging claim by claim, and it targets the rule change directly.
2. **Run the tool for one month on the desk's 40 claims,** and record every outcome. That gives us the proof we do not yet have.
3. **Ask Tanmay to separate "undecided" from "not fraud" in the old Zoho data,** and send us outcomes monthly so we can retrain.
4. **Ask Farhan** what a full inspection costs, so we can compare inspecting small claims from new partners against paying the fraud.

**What we are unsure about.** There are only 36 confirmed frauds since 1 May, so these numbers could move. We expect the real result on July to September claims to be a bit worse. Brand-new partners with no history are the hardest to judge. The tool will need retraining as fraudsters adapt.
